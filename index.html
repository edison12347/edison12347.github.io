calsync

A personal calendar sync tool by Eduard Chudaikin.

calsync keeps one person's work calendar (Microsoft Outlook) and personal calendar (Google Calendar) in step. It runs on its owner's own computer:

  • work meetings are copied into a separate Google calendar, title and time only;
  • busy personal events appear in the work calendar as private "Busy" blocks, without details.

It is not a public service. It has one user, its author, and no one else can sign up for it.
